- Editing an item automatically updates every caller, across owners.
- Updates continue through the full dependency chain.
- There are no pin/follow modes or explicit dependency upgrades.
- Propagation stays on the edited branch; main and sibling branches stay unchanged.
- Undo restores a previous version and updates its callers too.
- deps shows dependencies and callers; status shows what changed.
- Permission approval remains separate from code updates.
- Historical content stays addressable by its hash.
